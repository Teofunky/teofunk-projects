## Description (4000 chars)

Konnakol is the ancient South Indian art of vocal percussion — a spoken system of rhythmic syllables (Ta, Ka, Di, Mi, Ki, Tha, Tham, Dhin) that trains the inner ear to feel complex rhythmic structures. Used by master musicians to compose, communicate, and perform intricate rhythmic patterns, konnakol is the foundation of Carnatic music's rhythmic language.

**Konnakol Master** is the definitive app for learning this art, designed with professional musicians and students in mind.

### Your Personalised Learning Path

Work through 16 progressive levels, from single-syllable patterns to complex polyrhythmic korvais. Each level builds naturally on the last — Adi Tala first, then Rupaka, Misra Chapu, Khanda Chapu, and advanced compositions spanning multiple talas.

### The Mute Step: Train Like the Masters

The signature feature of Konnakol Master. After learning a pattern with the metronome, the metronome goes *silent*. You must maintain the pulse alone. The app listens to your voice, then shows you exactly how far each beat drifted from the grid — beat by beat, in milliseconds.

This is how real musicians train internalized rhythm.

### Sample-Accurate Metronome

A precision metronome engine with 24-beat lookahead scheduling. The click never drifts. Choose from five talas and five nadais (subdivisions), and mix and match to explore the full rhythmic grid of Carnatic music.

**Talas**: Adi (8 beats) · Rupaka (6 beats) · Misra Chapu (7 beats) · Khanda Chapu (5 beats) · Tisra Triputa (7 beats)
**Nadais**: Tisra (÷3) · Chatusra (÷4) · Khanda (÷5) · Misra (÷7) · Sankeerna (÷9)

### Voice Analysis

Speak into your device. Konnakol Master detects each syllable onset using spectral flux analysis, compares it to the expected beat grid, and scores your performance. Drift under 30ms is perfect. Under 60ms is good. The system adapts its sensitivity to background noise so it works in rehearsal rooms, home studios, or anywhere you practise.

### Practice Mode

A frictionless free-practice environment with the Tala Wheel — a visual beat clock where the current beat pulses with a saffron glow and your recent history fades behind it like a trail. Use the trikalam toggle to hear the tempo at half, normal, or double speed.

### Designed for Serious Students

- Dark-first display — optimised for performance lighting
- Monospaced syllable typography for precise visual alignment
- 8-point grid layout system for clarity on any screen size
- Haptic feedback on every beat change

### Progress Tracking

- XP and level system across all 16 lessons
- Daily practice streak with calendar-accurate tracking
- Achievements for milestone moments

### Privacy

Microphone is used only during active recording sessions. No audio is stored or transmitted. All analysis happens on-device.

## Review Notes for App Review Team

The app uses the microphone to detect voice onsets during practice sessions. The microphone permission is requested only when the user explicitly starts a recording session.

Test account: No login required — all features are accessible immediately after onboarding.

To test voice analysis: navigate to any lesson → Speak step → tap Start Recording → speak the displayed syllables rhythmically → observe drift score.

To test mute step: navigate to Level 1 → Mute Test step → tap Start → wait for metronome to go silent → continue speaking → observe per-beat drift chart.
