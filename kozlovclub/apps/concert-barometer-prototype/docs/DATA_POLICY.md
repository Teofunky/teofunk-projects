# Data policy

1. Facts, proxies, estimates, hypotheses and forecasts are stored/labeled separately.
2. Synthetic data is `kind=synthetic` and excluded from live-only mode.
3. Competitor availability is never called sales without an authorized transaction feed.
4. Material political/security events require verification; rumours may only enter as measured attention signals.
5. Forecasts must eventually be stored as immutable vintages and backtested without look-ahead leakage.
6. Automated collection must obey source terms, robots/rate limits and applicable law.
