- name is Liana (Лиана, Лианочка); birthday Oct 18, turned 78 on 2026-10-18, celebrating in Yerevan where she lives now
- youngest daughter in her family, doted on by her parents; small, graceful, beautiful
- lived in Yerevan near the Opera until about 45; studied at the Chekhov school; married Tigran ("Тигранчик") at 22; taught Russian language and literature at the Pushkin school
- two sons: Teofunk (family name "Айлюлик") and younger Rafael ("Рафулик")
- later moved to Košice, Slovakia, where she wrote poems and songs about her first granddaughter Anastasia and a fairy tale about "Зайчик Чик"; then moved to Moscow, now back in Yerevan
- Teofunk wrote a birthday song for her 78th ("Лиана, Лианочка")
