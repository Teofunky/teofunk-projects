- Teofunk is engaged in creative music production using Suno AI, focused on crafting high-quality, viral-ready tracks
- Core goal: natural-sounding, non-synthetic vocals and genre-authentic production across a wide stylistic range
- Teofunk has strong, clear aesthetic direction and references specific artists and tracks as sonic anchors

## Sonic reference anchors

- Noga Erez — "Knockout" (electronic original plus the "Against The Machine" live version)
- Ladaniva — "Jako"
- Buena Vista Social Club — "Chan Chan"
- Sin Susto — Cuban timba
- Bill Withers & The Crusaders — "Soul Shadows"
- Jamiroquai — acid-jazz funk
- Komitas-influenced Armenian folk music

## Recurring creative material

- Original Armenian folk lyrics featuring the refrain "Խիո, խիո, խանե, խանե" (Khio khio khane khane)
- This refrain is a recurring creative element, adapted across multiple genre treatments

## Current state

- Actively iterating on Suno prompts across several stylistic directions: dark alt-pop electro hip-hop, Cuban timba, children's symphonic jazz overture, Soul Shadows / Bill Withers style, and Jamiroquai acid-jazz funk
- These directions are built either around the Armenian folk material or as standalone sonic concepts
- The core challenge being navigated is achieving vocals that sound natural rather than AI-generated
