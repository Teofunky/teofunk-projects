- Building "Holler Box": guitar-triggered vocal instrument, voice reacts to note attacks in real time
- Three modes: 1) Hip-Hop — atonal rhythmic recitation phrases, US3 style; 2) Funky — syncopated percussive phrases/exclamations, James Brown style; 3) Melody — sung solo, pitch follows the note
- Wants manual mode switching (for now) and a separate phrase bank per mode
- Wants the app to support both uploading his own phrases/loops and recording new ones directly
- Found the syllable-chopping version choppy and unimpressive; after an A/B test prefers the new approach (talkbox-style voice for Melody, grid-quantized MPC-style phrases for Hip-hop), "намного лучше"
