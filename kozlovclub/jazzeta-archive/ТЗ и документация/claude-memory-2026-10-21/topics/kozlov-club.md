- Клуб Алексея Козлова (Kozlov Jazz Club) is at Маросейка 9/2, Moscow
- Has four stages: Главная сцена, The Beat, Мансарда, and Unplugged at Мясницкая 15
- Hosts approximately 175 concerts per month
- Brand system: coral red #cb3a2a, with stage color coding
- Current club website runs on WordPress
- Club founder and namesake Алексей Семёнович Козлов (b. 13.10.1935) died 25.09.2026; Teofunk knew him closely for many years
- The club's audience is mostly 25–45 years old
- Ticket prices shown on the club site are final: no service fee is added
- Has a 3D tour of every stage except Мясницкая, with a separate link for each stage
